## Research interests

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interests in:

- **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning:** improving complex reasoning through verifiable data and learning methods.
- **Vision-language models:** studying hallucination, visual perception, and chart understanding.
- **Truthfulness and interpretability:** understanding internal representations and developing methods to detect or mitigate unreliable model behavior.
